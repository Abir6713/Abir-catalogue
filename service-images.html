Image Sourcing & Placement

Starting at ₦80,000

What It Entails

Images are not decoration — they teach. A well-chosen diagram can explain in five seconds what three paragraphs struggle to say. Poorly placed images, however, distract, confuse, or sit so far from the relevant text that they lose all meaning.

We source the right visual for every concept, then place it precisely where the reader needs it — with a caption that adds to the teaching, not just labels the picture.

What's Included

  • Image sourcing: Photographs, illustrations, diagrams, charts, maps and infographics — all legally licensed for print.
  • Custom illustrations: Original diagrams created where a stock image won't do.
  • Placement markers: Every image tagged to the exact paragraph or section it belongs to.
  • Captions: Every image delivered with a figure number and a teaching caption.
  • Resolution check: All images verified at 300 DPI or higher for print quality.
  • Alt text: Descriptions for accessibility and for use in digital editions.
  • Reference list: Full source and licence information for every image used.

Our Process

Read through: We read the chapter and identify every point that would benefit from a visual.

Source or create: Stock images are downloaded; diagrams are drawn.

Place and caption: Each image is inserted as a marker in the manuscript with a figure number and caption.

Licence delivery: You receive a source list, so your publisher or printer can verify rights.

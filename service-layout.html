Page Design & Layout

Starting at ₦150,000

What It Entails

Layout is where a manuscript becomes a book. Words that read fine in a Word document can look cramped and unwelcoming on the printed page — and readers give up faster than you think. Layout fixes that.

We take your edited, corrected manuscript and design the page: margins, typeface, line spacing, column width, heading styles, image placement, page numbering, running heads, footnotes, and everything else that turns text into a readable, professional textbook.

What's Included

  • Page grid: A consistent layout system across the entire book.
  • Typeface pairing: A professional combination of body and heading fonts.
  • Margins and leading: Optimised for readability and safe for binding.
  • Heading hierarchy: Chapter, section, sub-section clearly visually distinguished.
  • Image integration: Images and captions placed so they sit with the text they explain.
  • Running heads & page numbers: Book title on the verso, chapter on the recto.
  • Widow and orphan control: So no page ends awkwardly.
  • Print-ready InDesign file: Or PDF, depending on your printer's needs.

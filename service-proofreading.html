Proofreading

Starting at ₦80,000

What It Entails

Proofreading is the final quality gate before your textbook goes to print. It happens after editing and after layout — once the pages are already designed and the words are in their final position.

At this stage, we are not rewriting. We are hunting. Every stray comma, doubled word, wrong page number, missing letter, or inconsistent capitalisation is caught and corrected. A textbook with a typo on page 47 loses credibility with the very students it's meant to teach — so we treat every page as if it's the one that will be opened to.

What's Included

  • Spelling & grammar: Every word checked against standard British or American English (your choice).
  • Punctuation: Commas, apostrophes, quotation marks, dashes — all verified.
  • Typographical errors: Doubled words, missing letters, transposed characters.
  • Consistency: Number formats, capitalisation, hyphenation, abbreviations.
  • Cross-references: Page numbers, figure references, table of contents accuracy.
  • Layout-level errors: Widows, orphans, misaligned headings, broken line breaks.
  • Two full passes: Because one pass always misses something.

Our Process

Pass 1 — Read-through: A full read for sense, catching the obvious errors.

Pass 2 — Line-level check: A slow, methodical pass looking at each line for subtle errors the first pass missed.

Query sheet: Anything ambiguous — an unclear term, an inconsistent figure — is flagged for your decision rather than guessed at.

Final delivery: A clean proofread file plus a marked-up version showing every change made.

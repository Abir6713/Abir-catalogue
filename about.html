About Abir Publishing

Quality textbooks, end to end

Who We Are

Abir Publishing is a full-service publishing house dedicated to producing high-quality academic textbooks. We work with authors, lecturers, institutions and academic boards — from the first idea through to the finished, bound book.

We exist because publishing a textbook is not a single job. It is a chain of specialised skills: planning, writing, editing, proofreading, design, prepress, printing, and binding. Most authors have to assemble that chain themselves, coordinating freelancers, printers, and designers — and quality slips at the joints. At Abir Publishing, the whole chain is under one roof.

What We Do

We offer fourteen distinct services across four stages of the publishing process:

  • Planning & Development: Topic and content planning, copywriting, content development, image sourcing.
  • Editorial & Quality Assurance: Editing, proofreading, manuscript preparation, corrections and revision.
  • Design & Prepress: Page design and layout, cover design, prepress preparation.
  • Printing & Finishing: Printing, finishing and binding, and final inspection.

Each service is available on its own — so if you only need proofreading, or only need a cover designed, you can hire us for that alone. Or you can hand us the entire project and we will carry it through from plan to print.

Our Approach

Clarity first. A textbook exists to teach. Every choice we make — from the vocabulary we use to the way a page is laid out — is driven by whether it helps the reader learn.

Academic rigour. We understand curricula, learning outcomes, and the demands of formal education. Our writers and editors are careful with facts, terminology, and the level of the audience.

Attention to detail. A book with a typo, a misaligned heading, or a badly bound spine loses credibility with its readers. Every stage of our process is designed to catch those problems before they reach print.

Full accountability. Because we handle the whole chain, we cannot pass blame to a supplier when something goes wrong. That makes us careful at every stage.

Who We Work With

  • Lecturers and academics writing textbooks for their own courses or for wider distribution.
  • Institutions and departments commissioning custom textbooks or course materials.
  • Independent authors publishing academic or educational works.
  • Publishers and academic boards outsourcing part or all of their production pipeline.

Our Commitment

Every project we take on — whether it is a single proofread or a complete 400-page textbook from scratch — receives the same standard of care. Our name goes on the finished product alongside yours. We do not take that lightly.

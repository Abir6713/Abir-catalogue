Editing

Starting at ₦120,000

What It Entails

Editing is where the substance of your textbook is sharpened. This is not a spell-check — it's a full review of structure, argument, clarity, tone, and academic consistency. We work at two levels: developmental editing (does the chapter make sense as a whole?) and line editing (is each sentence doing its job?).

By the time editing is done, your manuscript reads cleanly, argues clearly, and holds together from chapter one to the last page. Proofreading comes later — editing is what happens before the book is laid out.

What's Included

  • Structural review: Does each chapter flow logically? Are sections in the right order?
  • Clarity: Rewriting tangled sentences without changing your meaning.
  • Consistency: Terminology, tense, tone and formatting unified across chapters.
  • Academic register: Ensuring the writing matches the level of the audience.
  • Factual flagging: Anything that seems questionable is queried, not silently changed.
  • Developmental queries: Notes suggesting places where content could be added or trimmed.
  • Tracked changes: Every edit visible, so you keep full control.

Sample: How It Works

Below is a paragraph before and after line editing. The original is grammatically correct but wordy and unclear. The edited version says the same thing in fewer, sharper words.

Editing Sample — Chapter 3, Paragraph 1

Before

"In order to be able to understand the concept that is being presented in the chapter, the student needs to make sure that they read the chapter very carefully, and also that they take note of the important points which are being made by the author throughout the course of the discussion."

After

"To understand the concept presented in this chapter, students must read carefully and note the author's key points as the discussion develops."

Original: 54 words. Edited: 24 words. Same meaning — twice the clarity.

Our Process

Read-through: We read the full manuscript before changing anything, to understand its argument.

Developmental pass: Structural notes — what's missing, what's repeated, what's out of order.

Line edit: Sentence-by-sentence review with tracked changes.

Query sheet: Anything ambiguous is sent to you for a decision.

Clean delivery: You receive both the tracked version and a clean final version.

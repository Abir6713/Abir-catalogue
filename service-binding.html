Finishing & Binding

Custom Quote

What It Entails

Binding is what holds your textbook together — literally. It's the difference between a book that survives a semester and one that loses pages by week three. The choice of binding affects durability, how the book opens and lies flat, and how professional it looks.

We handle the full finishing process: trimming, folding, collating, and binding — in the style that suits your textbook's purpose and price point.

What's Included

  • Trimming: Pages cut to the final trim size.
  • Folding: Large sheets folded into page signatures.
  • Collating: Pages assembled in correct order.
  • Binding: Saddle-stitch, perfect binding, or hardcover — your choice.
  • Cover attachment: Cover wrapped and glued or sewn as needed.
  • Quality check: Every finished copy inspected.

Final Inspection

Starting at ₦40,000

What It Entails

Final inspection is the last set of eyes on your textbook before it goes out the door. A physical check of the finished copies — not the file, the actual book — to catch anything that slipped through earlier stages.

Printed copies can reveal problems that no file ever would: pages that were bound upside down, ink that didn't set properly, covers that were cropped too tightly, or signatures that were bound in the wrong order. This is where those get caught.

What's Included

  • Content check: Pages present, in the right order, no missing chapters.
  • Layout check: Margins, headings, page numbers all correct on the printed page.
  • Cover check: Correct title, subtitle, author, ISBN, barcode; no cropping errors.
  • Binding check: Pages secure, spine straight, no loose signatures.
  • Print quality check: Ink consistency, colour accuracy, no smudging or fading.
  • Sample copies: We inspect multiple copies from different parts of the print run.
  • Inspection report: A written report with pass/fail notes and photo evidence.

Our Process

Sample selection: We pull copies from the beginning, middle and end of the run.

Physical inspection: Every page turned; every feature checked.

Report: Written report delivered with photos of any issues.

Action: Any problems are resolved with the printer before final delivery.

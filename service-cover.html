Cover Design

Starting at ₦100,000

What It Entails

A textbook cover does three jobs at once: it tells the reader what the book is about, it communicates the level of the book (secondary, undergraduate, professional), and it survives looking at itself on a shelf or a thumbnail on a screen for years.

We design the front cover, spine, and back cover as a complete unit — with the title, subtitle, author name, publisher logo, ISBN, and barcode positioned correctly for printing.

What's Included

  • Three initial concepts: Three distinct design directions to choose from.
  • Front cover design: Title, subtitle, author, imagery.
  • Spine: Book title, author name, publisher logo, correctly measured for the book's thickness.
  • Back cover: Blurb, author bio, ISBN, barcode, publisher details.
  • Print-ready artwork: CMYK colour, correct bleed, 300 DPI.
  • Digital version: RGB version for online listings and social media.
  • Unlimited refinements on the chosen concept.

Content development is the actual writing of the textbook. This is the service you order when you have a subject in mind — but no manuscript yet.

Sample Page — Business Studies for Senior Secondary

Chapter 4: The Sole Proprietorship

A sole proprietorship is a business owned and managed by one person. It is the oldest and simplest form of business organisation. The owner provides the capital, takes all decisions, receives all profits, and bears all losses alone.

In Nigeria, sole proprietorships are everywhere — from the woman selling provisions at a market stall to the man running a small printing shop. Because there are no partners or shareholders to consult, decisions can be made quickly.

[ Illustration: A small shop owner at work ]
Figure 4.1 — A typical sole proprietorship: a small retail shop in Lagos.

Despite its simplicity, the sole proprietorship has real disadvantages. The biggest is unlimited liability: if the business owes money, the owner's personal property can be used to pay the debt.

Review Questions

  1. Define a sole proprietorship in your own words.
  2. List three advantages and three disadvantages of this business form.
  3. Explain what "unlimited liability" means using an example.

Content Development

Starting at ₦250,000

What It Entails

Content development is the heart of your textbook. We take the outline and scope defined in the planning stage and turn it into fully written, engaging chapters. Our writers work closely with you to ensure the voice, tone, and complexity of the material is perfectly suited for your target audience—whether that's primary school students, university undergraduates, or professional trainees.

We don't just write; we develop educational assets. This means creating end-of-chapter questions, case studies, diagrams, and practical examples that reinforce learning and meet curriculum standards.

What's Included

  • Drafting Chapters: Writing full chapters based on your approved outline.
  • Curriculum Alignment: Ensuring content matches national or institutional educational standards.
  • Educational Features: Creating exercises, summaries, and review questions.
  • Collaboration: Revisions and adjustments based on your feedback during the writing process.
  • Source Integration: Properly citing research and integrating academic sources accurately.

Our Process

We begin with a kick-off consultation to align on tone and expectations. From there, we draft chapters in phases, sharing them with you for review before moving to the next. This ensures the manuscript stays on track and meets your vision from the very first page.

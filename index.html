Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Answers to the questions authors ask us most often before they get in touch.

Do I keep the copyright to my textbook?
Yes. You retain full copyright of your work. We are a service provider — we do not claim any ownership of your manuscript, your content, or the finished book. We work on your project under your name, for your purposes.
Can I hire you for just one service, like proofreading?
Absolutely. Every service stands on its own. If you already have a manuscript and only need it proofread, we handle that alone. Same for layout, cover design, or any single stage.
How long does a full textbook take from start to finish?
It depends on the scope. A short secondary school textbook might take 8–12 weeks end to end. A large university-level textbook with new content development can take 5–8 months. We give you a specific timeline with your quote.
Do you work with authors outside Nigeria?
Yes. We work remotely with authors anywhere in the world. Manuscripts, edits and approvals are handled by email and WhatsApp. Printed copies are delivered to you through your preferred courier.
How is payment structured?
Typically 50% upfront to begin the work, and 50% on delivery. For larger multi-stage projects we can split payments per stage. Every quote is clear about what is included, so there are no surprises.
What if I'm not satisfied with the result?
Every service includes two full rounds of revision. If something is not meeting the brief, we fix it. Our aim is for you to be confident putting your name on the finished product alongside ours.
Will anyone else see my manuscript?
Only the team members actively working on your project. We treat every manuscript as confidential — this is standard practice in publishing, and we take it seriously.

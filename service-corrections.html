Corrections & Revision

Starting at ₦50,000

What It Entails

No manuscript is perfect on the first or second pass. Corrections and revision is the service that handles every change requested after the edit is done — whether the requests come from you, from a co-author, or from a lecturer or reviewer marking the manuscript for approval.

Crucially, we don't just apply changes. We track every correction against a request list, so you can see at a glance what was asked for and what was done. Nothing gets silently skipped.

What's Included

  • Lecturer feedback: Changes requested by academic reviewers, applied line by line.
  • Author revisions: Your own changes after the edit is returned.
  • Factual corrections: Errors in dates, figures, names, or terminology fixed.
  • Structural re-edits: Reordering or rewriting sections where requested.
  • Cross-reference updates: If a chapter moves, figures and page numbers adjust.
  • Change log: A document listing every requested change and its status.
  • Re-verification: After changes, we re-proofread to ensure no new errors slipped in.

Our Process

Receive request list: You send us the requested changes — email, marked PDF, or handwritten notes.

Apply changes: Every change made in tracked-changes mode.

Re-proof: A full pass to catch anything the changes introduced.

Delivery: Clean file + change log + tracked version.

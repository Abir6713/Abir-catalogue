What It Entails

Printing is where your textbook becomes something you can hold. But not all printing is the same — the choice of paper, ink, and press method affects cost, durability, and how the book feels in a student's hands.

We match the print method to your print run, your budget, and your book's purpose. Whether you need 100 copies for a single department or 5,000 for national distribution, we choose the right approach.

What's Included

  • Print method selection: Digital for short runs, offset for long runs.
  • Paper selection: Advice on stock weight, texture, and coating.
  • Colour printing: Full colour, spot colour, or black & white — whichever suits the content.
  • Cover printing: Gloss laminate, matte laminate, or soft-touch finish.
  • Quality verification: First copy inspected before the full run.
  • Delivery: Nationwide delivery of finished copies.

Our Process

Quote: You tell us copies, page count and paper preference; we give a firm price.

Proof: A single printed copy is produced for your approval.

Print run: Full production begins once the proof is approved.

Delivery: Finished copies boxed and delivered.

Manuscript Preparation

Starting at ₦70,000

What It Entails

Once every chapter has been written, edited and approved, they exist as separate files. Manuscript preparation is the service that turns those scattered files into one professional, submission-ready manuscript.

This is not just "combining files". It's ensuring the front matter is right, the chapters are numbered and ordered correctly, the page flow makes sense, and the whole document is clean enough to move into design and printing.

What's Included

  • Chapter sequencing: Arranged in the correct teaching order.
  • Front matter assembly: Title page, copyright page, dedication, acknowledgements, preface, table of contents.
  • Back matter: Appendices, glossary, bibliography, index.
  • Page numbering: Roman numerals for front matter, Arabic for the main content.
  • Heading hierarchy: Consistent chapter, section and sub-section formatting throughout.
  • Cross-references: Figures, tables and chapters referenced correctly by number.
  • One clean master file: Delivered in Word and PDF.

Manuscript Structure — Front to Back
Front Matter (Roman numerals: i–xii)
  • Title page
  • Copyright & ISBN page
  • Dedication
  • Acknowledgements
  • Preface / Foreword
  • Table of Contents
  • List of Figures & Tables
Main Body (Arabic numerals: 1–300+)
  • Chapter 1 through Chapter N
  • Each chapter opens on a right-hand page
  • Consistent heading hierarchy throughout
Back Matter
  • Appendix A, B, C…
  • Glossary of key terms
  • Bibliography / References
  • Index

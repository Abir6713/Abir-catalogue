Copywriting

Starting at ₦80,000

What It Entails

Copywriting is the craft of writing words that do something — sell a book, open a chapter, describe a subject in a way that makes someone want to read further. For a textbook, this means the back-cover blurb, the introduction, the author biography, the marketing description, and any promotional text that supports the book.

Academic writers often have deep subject knowledge but write copy that reads like a lecture. We turn that knowledge into language that attracts students, lecturers, and institutions — without ever dumbing it down or losing the academic weight.

What's Included

  • Back-cover blurb: The 100–150 word description that sells the book on a shelf or online.
  • Author biography: A professional, credible bio in third person.
  • Introduction copy: Opening pages that set the tone and invite the reader in.
  • Marketing description: Text for Amazon, publisher sites, and catalogues.
  • Social & promotional copy: Short, punchy copy for launch posts and announcements.
  • Tagline & subtitle: A precise subtitle that says what the book actually does.
  • Two rounds of revisions: We refine until the tone is right.

Sample: How It Works

Here is a typical back-cover blurb we received, alongside the rewrite we delivered. Notice how the second version says the same thing — but makes you want to open the book.

Copywriting Sample — Back Cover Blurb

Before

"This book is about financial accounting. It covers the basic topics in accounting that students need to know. It is written for students in universities and polytechnics. The book has many chapters and each chapter explains one topic. It also has questions at the end."

After

"Accounting is not about numbers. It's about decisions — and the people who make them.

From the double-entry principle to the final trial balance, Financial Accounting for Nigerian Students walks you through every concept with worked examples, real Nigerian case studies, and end-of-chapter problems that build real competence.

Written for undergraduates and polytechnic students, this is the textbook you will still be using long after the exam."

Draft: We write the copy in your authorial voice, not ours.

Revision: Two full rounds of revision based on your feedback.

Final: Copy delivered ready to paste into your cover, listing, or marketing material.
